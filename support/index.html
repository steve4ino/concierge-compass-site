Support

Need help with Concierge Compass, found a bug, or have a feature idea? We’d love to hear from you.

Contact us

Email info@concierge-compass.com and include:

• What you were trying to do
• What happened instead
• Your device model and iOS version (helpful, not required)

Subscriptions & billing

Concierge Compass Premium is billed through your Apple ID. You can manage or cancel your subscription any time in your device’s Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Account & data

You can delete your account and all associated data from within the app: Profile → Settings → Delete Account. For anything else about your data, see our Privacy Policy or email us.
